Our Story

Three generations.
One passion.

Bella Notte was born from a simple idea: bring the warmth of an Italian family table to our city. Founded in 2015 by the Rosetti family, our restaurant has become a destination for those who believe a great meal is about much more than food.

Our Executive Chef, Marco Rosetti, trained in Bologna and Florence before bringing his craft home. Every pasta is hand-rolled. Every sauce is made from scratch. Every dish is an act of love.
